🧸 Kazkar — Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026. Applies to the Android app "Kazkar" (package com.kazkar.kazkar).

In short: Kazkar collects no personal data at all — neither the child's nor the parent's. The child's name lives only on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no tracking.

1. Who we are

Kazkar is an app with illustrated Ukrainian children's stories where your child becomes the hero of the tale. It is built by an independent developer (Ukraine). Contact: studsovet.ogatrk@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

None. We do not collect, store on servers, share with third parties, or sell any information about you or your child.

3. What stays on your device

  • The child's name (optional) and age (optional) — used only to place the name inside the story text. Stored strictly locally on your device — the app never sends them over the network.
  • Settings (theme, language, read-aloud speed) and downloaded books — also local only.

Cloud backup of this data is disabled at the app level (allowBackup=false) — it does not even enter Google Backup.

4. Network: why the app uses the internet

The only network use is downloading new books (texts and illustrations) from our public static catalog (GitHub Pages). These are ordinary anonymous HTTPS requests without any personal data, identifiers, or cookies. As with any internet connection, the hosting provider (GitHub) technically sees the request's IP address in order to deliver content; we have no access to those logs and do not link them to any person.

5. Read-aloud

Stories are read aloud by your device's speech synthesizer (system TTS). The story text is not sent to our servers — we simply have none. Note: some system voices may synthesize speech online through their own vendor (for example, until an offline voice is downloaded) — that is a device setting; if you prefer, download an offline voice in your system text-to-speech settings.

6. Ads, analytics, purchases

  • There are no ads and never will be — this is a children's app.
  • There is no analytics or tracking — no third-party SDKs.
  • There are no accounts and no registration.
  • The current version has no purchases. If they appear (book unlocks for parents), they will sit behind a parental gate and this policy will be updated in advance.

7. Children's privacy

The app is directed to children and follows the Google Play Families policy. We collect no personal data from children (COPPA, GDPR-K). Settings and any external links (including this page) are hidden behind a parental gate — a simple challenge a child cannot pass on their own.

8. How to delete data

All data is local, so you are in full control: reset the child's profile in the parent zone (this removes the name and age), or uninstall the app — then nothing remains on the device. There is nothing for us to delete on servers, because nothing is ever sent there.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, when purchases appear), the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
